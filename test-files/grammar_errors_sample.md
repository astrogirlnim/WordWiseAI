# Grammar Error Sampler

Below is a collection of **intentionally incorrect** sentences organized by error type. Use these to test and validate grammar‑checking features.

---

### 1. Subject–Verb Agreement
- The new marketing **campaign have** exceeded all expectations.  
- Every piece of data **show** a different trend.  
- Neither the writer nor the designers **was** ready for launch.

### 2. Verb Tense Consistency
- Yesterday we **launch** the ad set and *are seeing* clicks already.  
- By the time the funnel went live, the copy *has changed* three times.  
- While she **writes** the headline, she *was checking* analytics.

### 3. Sentence Fragments
- Because the landing-page headline looked catchy.  
- Such as split-testing different CTAs.  
- After fixing the broken link in the email sequence.

### 4. Run-On Sentences
- The A/B test finished we still don’t know which variant won.  
- Our bounce rate dropped it isn’t low enough yet.  
- The client wants revisions the deadline is tomorrow we must hurry.

### 5. Comma Splices
- The copy sounds persuasive, it still needs a stronger CTA.  
- We boosted the post, the reach doubled overnight.  
- The analytics dashboard refreshed, the numbers surprised everyone.

### 6. Pronoun–Antecedent Disagreement
- Each marketer should review **their** funnel before launch.  
- The team updated the deck because **they** was outdated.  
- Neither of the templates kept **their** formatting.

### 7. Misplaced & Dangling Modifiers
- **While tweaking headlines**, the conversion rate suddenly improved.  
- We almost revised every paragraph **in the document**.  
- **After finishing the email**, the video ad was uploaded.

### 8. Faulty Parallelism
- The campaign aims **to increase clicks, lowering costs, and brand awareness**.  
- She is talented at **writing copy, to design graphics, and analytics**.  
- The checklist requires **proofreading, formatting, and to schedule**.

### 9. Double Negatives
- We **don’t need no** extra approvals.  
- The draft **isn’t hardly** ready for stakeholders.  
- They **couldn’t find nothing** wrong with the headline.

### 10. Article & Determiner Errors
- We launched **an** unique lead magnet yesterday.  
- She rewrote **copy** for landing page.  
- **The** ads in Facebook performed better than expected.

### 11. Preposition Misuse
- Let’s brainstorm **on** a compelling headline **in** tomorrow.  
- The form converts higher **between** mobile traffic.  
- He insisted **to** a complete redesign.

### 12. Wrong Word Form or Choice
- The CTA button is very **effectively**.  
- Our metrics are at a historic **peek** this quarter.  
- We must **except** the client’s feedback before publishing.
